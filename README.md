# Brake-Rotor-Re-Design-and-Brake-Duct-Concept
Redesigned brake rotors and custom cooling ducts using SolidWorks, and Ansys. Reducing inertia, improved thermal dissipation, and helped direct air flow over brakes and optimize rear wing aero. Navigated tight FSAE regulatory constraints from initial simulation through manufacturing.

## Purpose
Engineered custom brake rotors and aerodynamic cooling ducts for Ontario Tech Racing to resolve critical brake overheating during endurance conditions. By combining heat transfer principles, aerodynamic analysis, and regulatory compliance, the project successfully improved thermal dissipation while optimizing overall vehicle performance. The final brake rotor concept was implemented. The brake cooling ducts are in the process of being made regulation complaint and simulated. 

- Brake Rotor Redesign: Conducted thermal simulations using MATLAB and SolidWorks to evaluate multiple design iterations, ultimately reducing rotational inertia and overall weight while integrating custom wheel speed sensor mounts.

- Aero Cooling Ducts: Designed custom ducting and end plates using SolidWorks and ANSYS CFD to route cooling air directly to the brakes, simultaneously redirecting inboard wheel wake outboard to improve rear-wing aerodynamic efficiency.
